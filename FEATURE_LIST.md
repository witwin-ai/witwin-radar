# Radar Feature List

## Packaging And Platform Support

- Prebuilt CUDA 12.8 wheels for CPython 3.10-3.14 on Linux x86_64 and Windows x86_64.
- Each wheel includes one Python-independent LibTorch Stable ABI Dirichlet library targeting PyTorch 2.10 or newer; the same binary is CI load-tested with PyTorch 2.10, 2.11, and 2.12 across CPython 3.10-3.14.
- Native CUDA images cover compute capabilities 7.0, 7.5, 8.0, 8.6, 8.9, 9.0, 10.0, 10.1, and 12.0, with compute 12.0 PTX retained for forward-compatible Blackwell execution.
- Linux release wheels are repaired and validated as `manylinux_2_35_x86_64` artifacts before publication.

## Public API

- Propagation flow: compile the scene, freeze each leg once with `witwin.radar.propagation.ChannelPropagationAdapter`, reevaluate per frame, compose with `witwin.radar.paths.TwoWayComposer` or `DirectComposer`, and synthesize with `witwin.radar.synthesis.synthesize_fmcw_beat`
- Radar pose is controlled directly with `Radar(position=..., target=..., up=..., fov=...)` or `radar.set_pose(...)`
- Scene assembly uses `Scene.add_structure(...)`, `Scene.add_mesh(...)`, `Scene.add_smpl(...)`, and `Scene.add_structure_motion(...)`
- `SMPLBody` is owned by `witwin.radar.geometry` and remains exported through `witwin.radar`; shared core stays free of radar-only body-model dependencies.
- Optional per-structure motion is available through `Scene.add_structure_motion(...)` and `Scene.update_structure(...)`. Callers pass `TransformMotion` instances directly.
- Public string-literal API types: `DetectorType`, `SamplingMode`, and `MotionSampling`
- Low-level radar solver entrypoint: `Radar.chirp()`, `Radar.frame()`, `Radar.mimo()`, and `Radar.apply_noise()`
- REMOVED with the Dr.Jit ray tracer: `Tracer`, `fresnel`, `Radar.simulate(...)`, `Radar.simulate_group(...)`, and `radar.last_scene` / `last_trace` / `last_tracer`. Each removed name raises with a pointer to its replacement; none of them falls back silently. `TraceResult` survives and is exported from `witwin.radar.trace_result`.
- Composed round-trip rows record `join_mode` (`"direct"` or `"multipath"`), so which paths a result contains is a checked property rather than an inference from its shape
- `TwoWayComposer.compose(...)` and `DirectComposer.compose(...)` refuse a leg batch whose row count is not the frozen topology's, and `TwoWayComposer` refuses a `ScatterResponse` that evaluates to the wrong number of sites. A mismatched frame is a different topology, and the native join would otherwise gather past the end of its buffers and publish a plausible round trip.
- `witwin.radar.synthesis` exports the shared waveform input contract - `SynthesisPathBatch`, `SlowTimeMode`, `WaveformSpecProtocol`, `require_compatible` - alongside `FmcwBeatSpec`, `channel_phasor_to_beat_weight`, `synthesize_beat_rows`, `synthesize_fmcw_beat`, `OfdmCfrSpec`, `require_ofdm_compatible`, `synthesize_cfr_rows`, and `synthesize_ofdm_cfr`.
- `SynthesisPathBatch` carries the weight's PROVENANCE: whether it already contains the reference-frequency phase, free-space spreading, and transmit power, and whether it is frozen or refreshed across slow time. `require_compatible(batch, spec)` refuses any waveform spec that would apply one of those a second time, before any kernel launch. `SynthesisPathBatch.from_radar_paths(...)` maps a composed batch zero-copy with Channel's provenance; `SynthesisPathBatch.from_real_amplitudes(...)` embeds the legacy real-amplitude path as the complex special case and preserves a negative amplitude's sign as the 180-degree reflection flip it is.
- `frequency_response` / `frequency_offsets_hz` carry a WIDEBAND band through the whole chain: `ChannelPropagationAdapter(..., frequency_offsets_hz=...)` declares a tuple of frequencies in Hz, `RadarLegBatch` and `RadarPathBatch` publish a `[K, F]` complex64 response paired with its `[F]` grid, and `TwoWayComposer` composes `H_in(f_j) * S * H_out(f_j)` per column. A column at `df = 0` is bit-identical to the reference coefficient, so a band is a strict superset of the narrowband answer and the OFDM anchor `H[0][p][0] == C_rt` survives exactly. `row_valid` stays `[K]` and broadcasts: whether a stationary point exists is geometry, not frequency. Both legs must carry the band or neither. The scatter response is evaluated once and reused across the band, so a composed column is `H_in(f_j) * S(f_ref) * H_out(f_j)` - propagation and material band shape are exact, the target's is a declared deferral. Details and measurements: R-ADR-015.
- `synthesize_ofdm_cfr` indexes the band PER SUBCARRIER when the batch carries one, in the primal, the JVP and the VJP together. A wideband column already carries `exp(-j*2*pi*f_n*tau_rt)`, so the kernel applies only that phase's slow-time change - applying the full delay again would put every tap at twice its range. `OfdmCfrSpec.frequency_offsets_hz` is the single owner of the `n * df` mapping, so a caller wires the chain correctly by construction; `F` must equal `num_subcarriers`. Rule R8 now refuses a band only where it cannot be consumed: FMCW and pulsed LFM sweep continuously in fast time, have no discrete grid to index, and refuse it by name rather than discarding it.
- Wideband capability and its boundaries are measured rather than asserted. Accepted against closed forms: free-space spreading (`f_ref/f_n` tilt, 4.5e-5 relative), a lossy half space against single-interface Fresnel (9.6e-5), a 0.05 m slab across 2.4 Airy fringes against the transfer-matrix stack (9.6e-5), and a single-tap range profile against the inverse transform of the analytic band (3.3e-5). Refused with a number: a dispersive scene, because a frozen `DispersionSpec` record would drift `eps_r` by 32.7% over that sweep; a rough scene, because the Kirchhoff table is keyed on a frequency-hashed cache token; and a grid finer than one float32 step, because the offset does not exist.
- `WIDEBAND_FREQUENCY_RESOLUTION_PHASE_BUDGET_RAD = 0.1` is checked once per frozen topology, at freeze, where a host read is already paid. Channel publishes `native_frequency_resolution_hz` and the law `pi*resolution_hz*delay_s` but deliberately does not evaluate the bound, because that needs `max(delay_s)`. A topology whose delays exceed the budget is refused with both numbers named; there is no clamped or reduced-accuracy mode.
- `synthesize_fmcw_beat(batch, spec)` consumes a `SynthesisPathBatch` and calls `require_compatible` before any launch, so a weight and a spec that would count the carrier, the spreading, or the Doppler twice are refused rather than producing a plausible cube.
- `FmcwBeatSpec` carries `reference_frequency_hz`, `num_tx`, and `num_rx`, and derives `slot_period_s` and `max_unambiguous_speed_mps`. TDM-MIMO is synthesized natively: the slow-time coordinate of a sensor pair is its slot `chirp * num_tx + tx`, one chirp period apart, and `num_tx = 1` reproduces the pre-TDM output bit for bit.
- `synthesize_ofdm_cfr(batch, spec)` synthesizes an OFDM channel frequency response cube `H[symbol, sensor_pair, subcarrier]` from the same `SynthesisPathBatch`. `OfdmCfrSpec` pins subcarrier `n = 0` to the reference frequency, so `H[0][p][0]` is exactly the Channel coefficient, and derives `useful_symbol_time_s`, `symbol_period_s`, `waveform_sample_period_s`, `range_resolution_m`, and `max_unambiguous_speed_mps`. The cube is published in the CHANNEL phasor convention `exp(-j*k*d)` and is NOT conjugated, unlike the FMCW beat cube; both carry their convention as data (`OfdmCfrSpec.phasor`, `CHANNEL_PHASOR`). Phase-6 OFDM is narrowband by construction: the material and antenna response is frozen at the reference frequency across the band.
- `require_ofdm_compatible(batch, spec)` adds the cyclic-prefix contract to the shared provenance rules: a configured `max_expected_delay_s` that is not strictly less than `cyclic_prefix_s` is refused before any launch, because outside the CP window the single-tap per-subcarrier response gains an inter-symbol term the closed form does not have. There is no clamp and no reduced-accuracy mode. The bound is CONFIGURED, never a measured maximum delay, so the check costs no device-to-host transfer.
- The carrier-home rule - exactly one of `carrier_hz` and `carrier_rate_hz` may be nonzero - is one shared helper, `require_single_carrier_home`, used by every waveform spec rather than restated per waveform.
- `witwin.radar.propagation.kinematics` is the single seam from a Core `SceneSnapshot` to `(positions, velocities)` and to the ADR-038 forward duals that make `delay_rate` a propagation JVP rather than a finite difference. `rigid_site_velocities(...)` is `v(p) = v_cm + omega x (p - c)` about the instantaneous centre `rotation_centre_m(...)` names, `two_way_duals(...)` seeds the inbound and outbound legs together, and `DeformationVelocity` (`LinearDeformation`, `witwin.radar.geometry.SmplPoseDeformation`) supplies the deformation velocity Core does not carry.
- A velocity in `witwin.radar.propagation.kinematics` is a forward-AD tangent DIRECTION and never a leaf: `d(loss)/d(velocity)` does not exist in either AD mode, so `Kinematics`, `LinearDeformation` and any `DeformationVelocity` implementation REFUSE a velocity carrying `requires_grad` or a forward tangent before any object is built, naming ADR-038. A velocity derived from a grad-carrying position by `rigid_site_velocities` is refused by the same check; the supported workflow derives it from a detached copy of the positions and duals the live positions with the result, which keeps the position leaf and the tangent direction separate.
- `AspectScatterResponse.evaluate_rows` publishes a real geometry gradient: Channel's ADR-043 (consumer `CONTRACT_VERSION` 6) makes `PropagationGeometry.field_direction` graph-bearing for `{los, reflection}` under a frozen topology, so the `grad_dir_in` / `grad_dir_out` the native backward already computed now reach the endpoint positions, in both AD modes. Liveness is one decision for a whole propagation result, and the Radar adapter can only ever freeze components inside `capabilities().direction_differentiable_components`, so a partially live direction is unreachable from Radar.
- `SmplPoseDeformation` REFUSES a pose, shape or transform derivative instead of detaching it. The rest `Mesh` and every `DeformationState` it publishes cross the Core/Channel compile boundary and a pose derivative is not plumbed across it, so the old `vertices.detach()` in `rest_mesh` severed the graph silently and returned `grad = None`. `pose_rate` is refused as a leaf for the ADR-038 reason a velocity is: it is a tangent direction. `SMPLBody` itself is unchanged and still publishes differentiable vertices to the legacy radar `Scene`; the supported differentiable geometry for the Channel route is a `witwin.core.Mesh` vertex tensor.
- Scene-owned leaves reach a synthesized cube: a `witwin.core.Mesh` vertex tensor and a `PhysicalMaterial.eps_r` are both differentiable through compile, frozen-topology reflection reevaluation, the two-way join and FMCW synthesis, in reverse mode and (for the vertices) forward mode. A material-only forward dual is REFUSED by the adapter's dead-tangent guard, because a permittivity moves the coefficient and not the delay; a material forward tangent is available whenever the same call also carries an endpoint tangent. The in-plane vertex gradient is EXACTLY zero and that is the complete answer: a specular reflection depends on the facet's plane, not on its extent.
- `witwin.radar.propagation.SceneEpochLoop` owns the moving-world cadence: it samples a `DynamicScene` per frame, compiles only when the DECLARED structure descriptors move (endpoint motion never recompiles), rebinds through `refreeze` with the caller's `world_motion`, and publishes an `EpochFrame` whose `reason` names why a frame paid for a rediscovery - `first_frame`, `structure_motion`, `motion_event_cadence`, or `source_mutation`. On the motion-event tick it also rehashes the live world, so a scene mutated in place behind the compiled scene is recompiled and rediscovered by name instead of replaying silently; the per-frame cost stays two host integer comparisons per frozen handle.
- `witwin.radar.scattering.AspectScatterResponse` evaluates an aspect-dependent target response per path in a native kernel with primal, JVP and VJP, and DECLARES its aspect phase rate rather than measuring it. `witwin.radar.sigproc.microdoppler` is the Torch-side micro-Doppler analysis - slow-time spectrum, spectrogram, Doppler axis - and reaches no extension, per the post-processing directive.
- `witwin.radar.paths.RadarComponentIndex` names what each composed row IS - `target`, `environment_clutter`, `direct_leakage`, `multi_interaction` - from a caller-authored `ComponentDeclaration` (target sites, clutter sites, clutter material slots, multi-interaction depth). It is a SIDECAR: `RadarPathTopology` gains no column, so every component export shares the same topology OBJECT. It is built once per topology epoch from the frozen legs and reads no device value per frame. Every row must belong to EXACTLY one class; an undeclared site and a self-contradicting declaration are both refused at build rather than silently dropped. `join_mode` stays `{direct, multipath}` and the Phase-5 `hybrid` refusal is unchanged: the distinction it named is paid by a declaration over one topology, not by a third composer. Details: R-ADR-016.
- `witwin.radar.synthesis.select_component(batch, index, name)` exports one component by masking the synthesis WEIGHT with `torch.where`, exactly as a dead row already is, so a masked row is inert in the primal and carries no gradient. Row order, dtype, device, `row_valid` and the topology object are unchanged. Coherent recombination is plain summation of the per-component cubes and holds to `8 * eps_f32 * K * max|per-row contribution|` - not bitwise, because `(a + 0 + c) + (0 + b + 0)` is not `(a + b + c)` in float32; the measured residual is 3.25e-11 against a 2.48e-9 bound at `K = 11`. Exporting `n` components costs `n` synthesis launches and adds zero host observations and zero synchronizations.
- `witwin.radar.processing.combine_incoherent(cubes)` is the power-domain law `sum_j |cube_j|^2`, post-synthesis Torch, returning a REAL tensor. No waveform kernel gains an incoherent flag. A per-realization random phase in the scatter response is the physically honest alternative and is a declared deferral: it needs a native RNG and a seed contract consistent with the frontend's.
- `witwin.radar.propagation.ClutterComponentSpec{name, mobility, components, rediscovery_period_frames}` declares how a scene component moves - `static`, `replay`, `rediscover` - and `epoch_policy(specs, fixed_topology_components=...)` resolves a set of them into the one `world_motion` plus cadence a `SceneEpochLoop` takes. Mobility is a recorded CALLER DECLARATION and is never inferred, because whether a moving structure can gain a path is undetectable from inside a replay. A component whose propagation components are outside the consumer's `fixed_topology_components` must declare `rediscover` with a cadence; the refusal quotes the live capability record, so diffraction and transmission clutter cannot ride a replay.
- `witwin.radar.synthesis.assemble_frame_cube(cube, num_tx=..., num_rx=...)` packs the rank-3 `[chirp, sensor_pair, sample]` synthesis cube into the rank-4 `[TX, RX, chirp, sample]` layout every `sigproc` consumer indexes. It is waveform-neutral: an OFDM CFR cube packs the same way into `[TX, RX, symbol, subcarrier]`. The composed pair rank is SINK MAJOR (`pair = rx * num_tx + tx`, mirroring Channel's consumer) while the virtual antenna index is TX major, so the packing is a transpose; `pair_tx_index` / `pair_rx_index` invert the same numbering, and `validate_pair_ordering` checks it once at freeze time.

## Configuration

- `RadarConfig` frozen schema validates required radar fields and antenna layouts
- Optional `antenna_pattern` config defaults to a broadside dipole and also supports separable `x/y` 1D gain curves or a direct 2D gain map
- Optional `noise_model` config supports thermal noise, quantization noise, and phase noise with optional deterministic seeding
- Optional `polarization` config supports simplified TX/RX polarization vectors with alias strings (`horizontal` / `vertical`) or per-element 3D vectors
- Optional `receiver_chain` config supports `lna`, `agc`, and `adc` stages plus absolute TX-power scaling via `config["power"]`
- `Radar` accepts `RadarConfig` or raw config dictionaries
- `ChannelPropagationAdapter(compiled_scene, reference_frequency_hz=..., components=..., max_depth=...)` accepts `components={"los", "reflection"}` with `max_depth=1` for single-bounce multipath legs

## Native Kernels

- `two_way_join_forward` / `_backward` / `_jvp`: the inbound-by-outbound round-trip join, fused into one launch. The VJP reduces over frozen CSR segments with one thread per gradient slot, so it uses no atomics and its summation order is a property of the frozen join rather than of the schedule.
- `fmcw_beat_forward` / `_backward` / `_jvp`: FMCW beat synthesis over a chirp's fast-time axis. `segment_tx_index` and `num_tx` carry TDM slow time as kernel ARGUMENTS, so a MIMO frame is still exactly one launch.
- `ofdm_cfr_forward` / `_backward` / `_jvp`: OFDM channel frequency response over the `(symbol, subcarrier)` grid, one launch per frame. The subcarrier term multiplies the FULL delay while `carrier_rate_hz` multiplies the drift only, because a Channel coefficient carries the reference-frequency phase and nothing else; the rate derivative is therefore `-2 pi t_l (n df + f_c + f_r)` and not the delay derivative scaled by slow time.
- `sensor_weight_forward` / `_backward` / `_jvp`: the Radar-owned map from array geometry to a path weight - round-trip length and delay, the delay rate, the interpolated antenna pattern, free-space spreading, transmit amplitude, and the legacy receive projection - in ONE launch per frame. The three mode flags come from the path batch's provenance, so a Channel-sourced weight physically cannot have spreading, transmit power, or a polarization projection applied a second time. The backward uses no atomics: the antenna gradient is reduced over ascending rows with explicit scratch.
- `frontend_noise_forward` / `_backward` / `_jvp`, `frontend_agc_forward` / `_backward` / `_jvp`, `frontend_quantize_forward`: the receive chain in a fixed six-stage order with one ADC. Noise draws are counter-based Philox keyed by `(seed_base, stage_id, linear index)`, so the realisation is independent of block size and toggling one stage leaves every other stage bit-identical. `frontend_quantize_forward` is deliberately non-differentiable and raises on a grad-enabled or forward-dual input.
- `mimo_linear_backward` / `mimo_linear_jvp`: the AD companions of `forward_mimo_linear_chunked`. With them the fused TDM-frame launch is the only implementation of the frame path in every AD mode; the Torch expression that used to serve a reverse-mode call is deleted.
- `forward_chunked` / `forward_mimo_linear_chunked` / `dirichlet_jvp` / `mimo_linear_backward` / `mimo_linear_jvp` / `backward` / `backward_batched` / `backward_parallel_bins` / `backward_per_bin`: the Dirichlet range spectrum, owned in Python by `witwin/radar/synthesis/dirichlet_spectrum.py`. The path weight is COMPLEX, carried as two real tensors so no complex tensor crosses the autograd boundary; a real weight is the value `a_im = 0` and is bit-identical to what the family produced before it gained the component. `fc` selects the carrier home exactly as `carrier_hz` does in the beat family, and `tau_is_seconds` lets a caller pass a round-trip delay instead of a one-way distance.
- Every registered operator has exactly one Python owner, a direct contract test, and a production end-to-end caller, recorded in `ci/native-binding-manifest.json`.

## Solver Execution

- Native Dirichlet runtime state lives on `radar.solver`, including FFT metadata such as `pad_factor` and `N_fft`
- `Radar(device="cuda")` validates CUDA availability explicitly. CPU construction is supported for configuration and helper workflows, but solver execution requires CUDA tensors.
- Linux and Windows are supported targets. GitHub Actions builds Linux wheels
  inside manylinux_2_28 and Windows wheels on Windows Server 2022. Release
  fatbins include native SM87 SASS alongside the maintained CUDA 12.8
  architecture set; source builds require a CUDA-enabled PyTorch build plus a
  working CUDA/C++ compilation toolchain.
- Time-domain outputs from `Radar.chirp()`, `Radar.frame()`, and `Radar.mimo()` automatically apply `noise_model` when configured; `radar.mimo(..., freq_domain=True)` rejects built-in noise injection
- Time-domain outputs from `Radar.chirp()`, `Radar.frame()`, and `Radar.mimo()` automatically apply `receiver_chain` when configured; enabling it also moves the transmit amplitude onto an absolute transmit-voltage scale. `Radar.gain` is REMOVED: the transmit amplitude reaches physics only through the native sensor-weight owner's `tx_power_mode`, and `Radar.transmit_amplitude` is the value it applies
- `receiver_chain.adc` and `noise_model.quantization` are mutually exclusive so only one ADC quantizer is active
- MIMO frames sample the scene once per TDM chirp slot (`chirp_per_frame * num_tx` evaluations), so the velocity-dependent per-TX phase removed by `_compensate_tdm_phase` is physically present; moving-target elevation is now correct instead of biased by radial velocity
- `radar.mimo(interpolator, ...)` batches all TDM slots into grouped native kernel launches for roughly 8-16x faster dynamic-frame generation
- Public chirp and MIMO autograd uses analytical native CUDA backward kernels; float64 PyTorch implementations remain validation references only
- `motion_sampling="linear"` uses two triangle traces plus the fused range-rate MIMO kernel for fast rigid-motion approximation

## Rendering And Dynamics

- `Scene.compile_renderables(time=...)` exposes time-dependent geometry for dynamic scenes
- Multipath is discovered once per frozen topology and replayed per frame; a row that stops existing at new endpoint positions is published through `row_valid` with an exactly zero payload, as data rather than as an error
- The Dirichlet solver consumes generalized path samples; the total `tx -> bounces -> scatter -> rx` length, the antenna pattern, free-space spreading, and the polarization projection are evaluated in the native `sensor_weight` kernel rather than in Torch, and the solver carries a ROUND-TRIP DELAY in seconds rather than a halved distance
- When `polarization` is configured, traced path normals are propagated through the runtime and used for simplified reflection/projection coupling
- Shared core geometry constructors default to `device=None`, while radar `Scene(...)` owns device placement and defaults to CUDA
- `Timeline.from_motion()` uses the trace-result contract directly
- Dynamic structure motion supports rigid `translation`, `rotation`, and `parent` inheritance so rotational Doppler can be modeled directly from the scene
- `radar.mimo(..., freq_domain=True)` remains available for Dirichlet frequency-domain output

## Waveform Synthesis

- Three waveform owners consume ONE `SynthesisPathBatch`: `synthesize_fmcw_beat` (beat cube, conjugated), `synthesize_ofdm_cfr` (channel frequency response, Channel convention), and `synthesize_pulsed_echo` (received pulse train, Channel convention). `Radar.synthesize(paths, slow_time_mode=...)` dispatches on the STORED `waveform.kind`; a kind with no owner is a hard error with no fallback.
- `SynthesisPathBatch` carries the weight's PROVENANCE - whether it already contains the reference-frequency phase, free-space spreading, and transmit power - plus a `slow_time_mode` enum, and every waveform spec is validated against it before any kernel launch. Six documented double-count hazards become four unrepresentable states.
- `witwin.radar.sensors` is the single owner of array geometry, antenna pattern, transmit power, and the legacy receive projection. Transmit power reaches physics through `RadarEndpointSpec.powers_w` on a Channel-sourced path and through the sensor-weight kernel's `tx_power_mode` on the legacy real-amplitude path, never both.
- `witwin.radar.frontend.FrontendChain` is the single receive chain: port conversion, phase noise, input-referred thermal noise, LNA, AGC, and ADC, in a fixed order with one ADC and one seed base. It replaces the caller-ordered `noise_model` / `receiver_chain` pair.
- `ScalarRcsResponse.from_rcs(sigma_m2, reference_frequency_hz=...)` builds the target strength through the pinned law `S = sqrt(4 pi sigma) / wavelength`, so `|C_rt|^2` is the bistatic radar equation rather than a relative level. `sigma_m2` may be a 0-dim TENSOR, in which case the cross section is a differentiable leaf and the amplitude carries its graph: the canonical inverse-design question, reaching a synthesized cube in both AD modes. The `sqrt` is result construction, off the per-path loop, so no physics moved into Torch. `requires_grad=True` beside a tensor cross section is refused, because the leaf is `sigma_m2` itself.
- Every configuration scalar on every waveform spec (`FmcwBeatSpec`, `OfdmCfrSpec`, `PulsedEchoSpec`, `DirichletSpectrumSpec`) and every frontend stage spec (`PortSpec`, `NoiseSpec`, `LnaSpec`, `AgcSpec`, `AdcSpec`) is a host float and REFUSES a tensor at construction, naming the field, its owner and why that owner has no derivative slot. The rule is on the TYPE rather than on `requires_grad`, because a tensor that does not require grad today is the input that starts requiring grad tomorrow and `float()` on a device tensor is a host synchronisation as well. `witwin.radar.host_parameters.require_host_float` is the single owner. Previously each of these silently swallowed a marked tensor, ran the frame, and returned `grad = None`.
- `SensorWeightGeometry` and `SensorWeightPlan` refuse a `requires_grad` or dual-carrying value in any of their thirteen frozen constants - the three velocity sets, the fixed leg length, the facet normals, both polarization sets, the local frame and the five pattern tables - at construction, before `validate` and before any launch. They were documented as constants and returned `None` from `backward` by construction; the six differentiable inputs (`tx_pos`, `rx_pos`, `site_in`, `site_out`, `intensity`, the complex weight) are unchanged. A velocity additionally has no leaf semantics at all under ADR-038, and its message says so.
- `MimoPathCache` publishes `total_delay_s` (round trip, seconds), a COMPLEX `amplitudes`, and `delay_rate`.

## Signal Processing

- `witwin.radar.processing` is the processing facade: everything in it is PyTorch by owner directive, it consumes synthesis results, and it never mutates a path batch or changes composed row identity
- `ProcessingAxes.from_synthesis(result, spec, array)` is the ONE metadata / axes / units record all three waveforms share. It is built from the SI waveform specs, never from the flat engineering-unit `RadarConfig`, and it publishes `range_m` and `velocity_mps` as float64 SI axes plus a `units` mapping
- The cross-waveform Doppler sign is fixed as data. `ProcessingAxes.doppler_sign` is DERIVED from the cube's published `phasor` (`+1` for the conjugated FMCW beat cube, `-1` for the OFDM and pulsed Channel-convention cubes) and is applied exactly once, inside `range_doppler`. The canonical convention is `PROCESSING_DOPPLER_CONVENTION`: a positive Doppler bin is a CLOSING target, in every waveform
- `ProcessingAxes.as_fmcw_axes()` is the mechanical migration to the Phase-6 `RadarAxes`; it is refused for OFDM and pulsed, whose vocabulary that record cannot name
- `ProcessingCube.from_synthesis(result, axes)` attaches the chain to `SynthesisResult` through the existing `assemble_frame_cube` sink-major to tx-major transpose; there is no second packer
- `range_profile(cube, window=, remove_dc=)` is one entry with three backends selected by `axes.waveform`: an FMCW beat transform, an OFDM subcarrier INVERSE transform (the CIR, which did not exist before), and a pulsed matched filter without the legacy unconditional `complex128` upcast. It replaces three separate range FFTs that had three different windowing choices
- The fast-time mean subtraction `process_rd_tensor` applied unconditionally is now the explicit `remove_dc` flag and DEFAULTS TO OFF, so a clutter component export is not silently altered
- Every processing stage is amplitude normalised and rank generic: an isolated path row peaks at `|C_rt| * window_coherent_gain` in all three waveforms, and a `[TX, RX, C, S]` cube and a `[C, S]` slice give the same answer with no Python loop
- `beam_cube(rd, steering, directions=)` forms the beam / velocity / range cube, and `conventional_steering(array, directions)` builds the array manifold in the CUBE's own phasor convention, so a conjugated beat cube is not steered at the mirror-image angle
- `process_pc(..., detector=...)` accepts the validated detector set `{"cfar", "topk"}`
- `frame2pointcloud(...)` requires a `radar` argument so TDM-MIMO compensation is never skipped silently
- `PointCloudProcessConfig` provides the normalized point-cloud extraction config surface
- `ArrayGeometry` is the ONE statement of where a virtual element is: `[P, 3]` float64 metres, built once per array from a DECLARED `element_spacing_m`, so no half-wavelength spacing is written into any source file. It is TX major, matching the `[TX, RX, ...]` cube `assemble_frame_cube` publishes, and it carries the `phase_sign` that reconciles a conjugated beat cube in SPACE
- `conventional_steering(array, directions)` and `mvdr_weights(covariance, steering, diagonal_loading=)` are the two weight families. Both satisfy `w^H a = 1`, so they are interchangeable at `beam_cube` with no scale factor between them. The MVDR loading is REQUIRED and is a fraction of `trace(R) / P`, so it is scale free; the solve is `torch.linalg.solve`, never an explicit inverse
- `tdm_compensate(aoa_input, velocities, array, axes)` removes the TDM slot phase in ONE broadcast multiply, takes the canonical closing-positive velocity, and reads the RAW chirp period `slow_time_period_s / num_tx`
- `phase_comparison_aoa`, `fft2_aoa` and `music_spectrum` / `music_image` take an `ArrayGeometry` instead of a legacy `Radar`, and the route is named by the CALLER. All three reconcile the phasor, so a conjugated beat cube is not reported at its mirror image. MUSIC reads `array.spacing_wavelengths` and builds its spatial smoothing with `unfold`
- `ca_cfar`, `ca_cfar_fast` and `os_cfar` take `[..., D, R]` with an arbitrary leading batch, so a `[B, D, R]` beam cube needs no Python loop over beams, and return a `Detections{mask, threshold}` record whose `count` is a DEVICE scalar. `ca_cfar_1d` is the range-only detector a range profile never had. The published law is `P_fa = (1 + alpha / N) ** (-N)`, measured against an empirical rate rather than asserted
- `point_cloud(detections, rd, axes, array)` is ONE pipeline with the detector as an argument, publishing `PointCloud{xyz, velocity_mps, energy, range_m}` in float64 SI. Range gating is an explicit half-open pair in METRES read against `ProcessingAxes.range_m`; `max_points` thins the MASK, before the row list exists, so every column of a point comes from one cell
- `DetectionFrame`, `TrackHandoff` and `nearest_neighbour_associator` are the frame-to-frame handoff contract plus ONE reference constant-velocity associator. The whole handoff is explicitly non-differentiable and REFUSES a derivative-carrying input, in BOTH AD modes, rather than detaching it silently. `DetectionFrame.as_fixed_size(size, generator=)` is the fixed-shape batching helper, in torch with an explicit generator
- `witwin.radar.sigproc` keeps every public name as a migration adapter over this facade and contains no DSP of its own: after the Phase-8 cutover every production `torch.fft`, detector, angle estimator and beamformer in the radar tree lives under `witwin/radar/processing/`. The adapters preserve the legacy behaviour deliberately, including the symmetric window, the unnormalised transforms and the unreconciled Doppler sign, and each carries a `DeprecationWarning` naming its replacement
- `process_pc_tensor(...)` and `process_rd_tensor(...)` keep outputs on-device; `process_pc(...)` and `process_rd(...)` are NumPy compatibility wrappers
- `ca_cfar_2d(...)`, `ca_cfar_2d_fast(...)`, and `os_cfar_2d(...)` return `(detections, threshold_map)` with a consistent CFAR contract
- `ca_cfar_2d(...)`, `ca_cfar_2d_fast(...)`, and `os_cfar_2d(...)` all accept NumPy arrays and PyTorch tensors; the reference CA/OS paths now stay on the torch device instead of falling back to CPU
- The NON-DIFFERENTIABILITY WALL is enforced, and it sits at the first DISCRETE DECISION rather than at "post-processing". Above it `range_profile`, `range_doppler`, `beam_cube`, `matched_filter`, `tdm_compensate` and the MUSIC pseudo-spectrum are linear or smooth and stay differentiable, each with a liveness pin; the MUSIC spectrum's gradient is validated against a central difference. Below it `ca_cfar`, `ca_cfar_fast`, `os_cfar`, `ca_cfar_1d`, `point_cloud`, its `topk` peak selection, `phase_comparison_aoa`, `fft2_aoa` and `DetectionFrame` REFUSE a derivative at their entry, in both AD modes, before any device operation runs - measured, with the stage's own primitives replaced by counting stand-ins. `witwin.radar.ad_contracts.refuse_derivative` is the single owner and the frontend ADC guard now routes through it, so there is one wording and one behaviour. Before this, CFAR published a live threshold gradient, `point_cloud` published live `xyz` and `energy`, the angle estimators kept the tape across an `argmax`, and the tracking guard missed forward duals entirely
- Radar publishes FIRST DERIVATIVES ONLY. `witwin.radar.ad_contracts.first_order_only` decorates all ten registered `backward` methods and fails a second-order request loudly, naming the owner, before any second-order work: `create_graph=True` is caught by the grad-mode state it leaves enabled, and a cotangent that carries a forward tangent or its own graph is caught on the way in. Nested forward levels stay Torch-owned and are pinned rather than wrapped. This matches Channel's ADR-043 convention exactly, including the half Channel dropped - a forward dual on a `requires_grad` primal is a legitimate first-order request and is not refused. Previously `create_graph=True` succeeded everywhere and the failure appeared one step later as a generic Torch message or, with `allow_unused=True`, as a silent `None`
- The vendor DSP primitive surface is FROZEN and asserted by equality in four cells: transforms `torch.fft.{fft, ifft, fft2, fftshift, ifftshift, fftfreq}`; pooling and patch extraction `torch.nn.functional.{avg_pool2d, unfold, pad}`; decompositions and solves `torch.linalg.{eigh, solve}`; order statistics and selection `torch.{argsort, argwhere, gather, sort, topk, where}` plus `Tensor.{cumsum, index_select, unfold}`. Elementwise arithmetic, shape manipulation, contraction and random sampling are ordinary tensor operations and are explicitly out of scope; the six adjacent calls a reader could mistake for DSP are recorded separately, also by equality. `torch.linalg.inv` / `pinv` and the vendor window constructors are asserted ABSENT
- The native-DSP gate was MEASURED and the recorded answer is NO native DSP. Of the four criteria only dispatch-bound is tripped - every stage costs the same wall time on a 48x larger cube - and a cuFFT wrapper trades one dispatch for another rather than removing a launch. The per-criterion numbers are in `docs/dev/standards/radar-adr-017-processing-facade-and-frozen-dsp-surface.md`
- `tools/benchmark_processing.py` measures every stage and the full pipeline with the repository's one timing convention (CUDA events, explicit synchronize, medians, `--json`), at the frozen fixture size and at one realistic size. `tests/support/dsp_ledger.py` counts `torch.fft` dispatches, the four host-observing tensor methods, `torch.cuda.synchronize`, and separately the data-dependent-shape calls (`argwhere`, `nonzero`) that synchronize without naming it
- The full-pipeline budgets are FROZEN with their measured numbers and headroom factors written into `tests/test_phase8_pipeline_budget.py`: 2.23 ms latency at `x1.30`, 1.13 MB peak allocation delta at `x1.25`, exactly 1 host observation (the point cloud's `argwhere`, which IS the stage), exactly 7 `torch.fft` dispatches, and `os_cfar`'s 138 MB memory outlier at `x1.25`. The wideband budget is flat in the frequency column count: 1 D2H copy and 1 synchronization per leg, and `1 + F` join launches, at `F` in {1, 8, 64}
